- (direction by user) Create a course-demo UX for the Basys3 drawing-and-physics puzzle; primary path should be quick to understand, reliable on one integrated bitstream, and visibly demonstrate physics.
- (decision by user) Mouse is the only game input, including lobby, drawing, pause menu, retry, and return to lobby; course staff confirmed this exception.
- (decision by user) Clicking a stage starts immediately; the upper-left pause affordance is a two-bar pause icon; pause opens Resume, Reset Stage, and Back to Lobby.
- (decision by user) A released stroke becomes a movable object under gravity and collisions; the MVP includes rotation and angular collision response.
- (decision by user) Pause freezes the active attempt and resume continues it; reset or leaving for lobby restores the stage; win occurs on any ball-target overlap and waits for player choice.
- (note by coach) VGA is the gameplay and mouse interaction surface; Pmod OLED shows stage, objective, strokes remaining, and status; LEDs communicate status.
