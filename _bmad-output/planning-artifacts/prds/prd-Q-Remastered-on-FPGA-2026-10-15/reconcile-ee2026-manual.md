## Manual constraints and evaluation points

- Alternative I-FDP themes are allowed; the same criteria apply: functionality, robustness and reliability, user interface, and user experience. All claimed components must be integrated into one project/bitstream, with meaningful individual responsibilities and no separate-component demo.
- Basys3 and OLED are compulsory. Page 10 lists switches and LEDs as compulsory UI; page 11 explicitly allows a mouse. Mouse-only control has been confirmed by course staff per the user's prior clarification, so the PRD's exception is consistent with that clarification but differs from the manual's default switch requirement. LEDs and OLED remain included.
- Optional devices do not earn marks by themselves. The manual names VGA-to-HDMI as an available lab device, subject to the stated access conditions. PRD/addendum correctly leave the VGA path to be confirmed and focus evaluation on the game capability.
- Week 13 demonstration should take 2–5 minutes on average; assessors may skip hard-to-reach features. The PRD's short, direct core-loop goal reflects this.
- The final archive must contain one successfully generated and board-tested bitstream, be no more than 500 MB absent prior approval, and be checked after download/unzip. The report must describe only implemented, demonstrable capabilities; it has a strict four-page structure, team-size-based word limits, colour layout requirements, and mandatory references/documentation for open-source code and AI/code-generator use.
